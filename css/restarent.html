Idly

Price: 20

Idly is a traditional South Indian breakfast dish made from fermented rice and urad dal (black gram) batter. It is soft, fluffy, and typically served with coconut chutney and sambar.

Dosa

Price: 30

Dosa is a popular South Indian breakfast item made from a fermented batter of rice and urad dal. It is typically served with coconut chutney and sambar.

Vada

Price: 25

Vada is a deep-fried snack made from urad dal (black gram) and spices. It is often served with coconut chutney or sambar.

Upma

Price: 20

Upma is a savory South Indian breakfast dish made from semolina (rava) cooked with vegetables, spices, and sometimes nuts. It is often served with coconut chutney or pickle.

Chapathi

Price: 30

Chapathi is a type of unleavened flatbread made from whole wheat flour. It is a staple in Indian cuisine and is often served with curries, vegetables, or lentils.
